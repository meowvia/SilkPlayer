1. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW & LICENSING BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SilkPlayer employs a modular, decoupled architecture:

1. Host Application & Frontend (SilkPlayer):
   - License: GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 (LGPL-3.0)
   - Scope: User interface, window management, playback controls, settings,
     and open-source application shell.
   - Repository: https://github.com/meowvia/SilkPlayer

2. Media Demuxing & Decoding Frameworks (FFmpeg & dav1d):
   - Linkage: Dynamic Frameworks (Libavcodec, Libavformat, Libavutil,
     Libswresample, Libswscale) loaded via @rpath.
   - Licenses: GNU LGPL-3.0 (FFmpeg) and BSD 2-Clause (dav1d).
   - Relinkability: Users may replace or relink these dynamic frameworks in
     accordance with Section 4 of the GNU LGPL-3.0.

3. Core Playback & Metal Rendering Engine (SilkCoreKit):
   - License: SilkCoreKit Proprietary Binary License Agreement (meowvia).
   - Scope: Closed-source core engine decoupled via SPM binaryTarget.
   - Commercial Licensing & OEM Inquiries:

     licensing, third-party software integration, and enterprise licensing.
